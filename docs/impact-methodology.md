# North Carolina AI restrictions: source register

[Back to the Cloak README](../README.md#built-in-north-carolina) · [Machine-readable register](assets/nc-restrictions.json)

Reviewed October 26, 2026. 49 named organizations: 10 government · 1 business · 9 k–12 education · 27 higher education · 2 healthcare. North Carolina organizations with published AI-specific privacy limits, including policies, official guidance and recommendations, approved-tool requirements, supplier rules, and a clinical AI-device restriction. Not a census or a count of blanket bans. One entry per named organization, not per rule, school, department, branch, or facility. Department-specific rules are labeled and counted once under their parent organization. State agencies are included only with separately cited publications; no other agencies are inferred from statewide coverage. The UNC System Office is counted only for its own administrative operations, separately from the universities. Advocate Health and its Atrium Health operations count as one organization.

## If everyone used Cloak

If every student represented by the 267,382 enrollments used ChatGPT with Cloak: 267,382 × 4.28 prompts/day × 8.5% = approximately 97,274 sensitive prompts per day, or 35,504,854 per year at the same daily rate. An illustrative full-use scenario, not observed adoption or measured prevention. It treats each enrollment as one user, assumes institutionally permitted use, and holds both study rates constant. The population is not deduplicated. Cloak can reduce exposure of identifiers it detects; the share of these sensitive messages it could fully protect has not been measured. This is not a compliance guarantee.

Epoch AI reports 4.28 prompts per calendar day among weekly active panelists in July 2025, from a selected US YouGov panel of 5,000 people who used ChatGPT in 2026. Harmonic found sensitive information in 8.5% of tens of thousands of business prompts across five AI tools in Q4 2024. Its nearly-one-in-ten finding covers customer, employee, legal/financial, security and code data, not PII alone. Combining these different populations gives a scenario, not a measured NC student rate. Annual volume uses 365 days and is rounded only after multiplication. No byte-size or detection-success assumption is made.

Sources: [Epoch AI usage methodology](https://epoch.ai/data/chatgpt-usage-documentation/methodology) · [Harmonic sensitive-data study](https://www.harmonic.security/resources/new-research-the-data-leaking-into-genai-tools)

## Scope and exclusions

No count is assigned to unnamed organizations, academic-integrity-only bans, or historical media reports without sufficiently current qualifying evidence. Recommendations and limited-scope rules are identified explicitly. Inclusion does not imply Cloak can satisfy or remove a rule: approved-tool, clinical-device, and other requirements remain applicable. Listed organizations are not represented as Cloak customers, partners, endorsers, or adopters.

[Truist confirms its Charlotte headquarters.](https://www.truist.com/who-we-are/about-truist) Guidance pages and conditional approvals are included as restrictions; their inclusion does not mean a legally binding or organization-wide ban. No global employee or customer totals are presented as North Carolina populations. Enrollment figures do not represent observed ChatGPT or Cloak users. Full use is assumed only in the labeled scenario.
